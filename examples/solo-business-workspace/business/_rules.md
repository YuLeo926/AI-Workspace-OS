# Business Rules

These rules apply to the fictional example workspace.

## Data Safety

- Use fictional business examples only.
- Do not include real account names, order IDs, SKUs, customers, payment details, screenshots, or platform state.
- Keep public examples generic.

## Work Rules

- Read the workspace index and this week file before acting.
- Keep handoff files short.
- Write durable decisions as ADRs.
- Log audit-worthy actions with rollback hints.
- Treat cross-owner writes as suggestions unless delegated.

## Folder Rules

- `product/` contains fictional product planning.
- `content/` contains fictional content planning.
- `client-work/` contains fictional client workflow examples, not real client data.
- `research-backlog/` contains generic ideas and research questions.
